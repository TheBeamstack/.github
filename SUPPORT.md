# Support

## Community projects (Bunyan, Road-Designer, …)

- **Questions & discussion:** open a Discussion, or an issue on the relevant
  project repository.
- **Bugs:** open an issue with a minimal reproduction, the project version, and
  your environment.
- **Feature ideas:** open an issue describing the problem you are trying to
  solve, not just the feature.

Community support is best-effort and unpaid.

## Commercial products (Miqdar, Maître d'Ouvrage, …)

Support is provided under your commercial agreement — use the channel named in
that agreement, or **askdaoudi@gmail.com**.
